# EXERCISE 1 — Seated Cable Row

## A. Identification
**App name after audit:** `חתירה בכבל בישיבה` (unchanged)
**Previous app name:** `חתירה מלמטה בכלוב` → renamed in migration 3
**Exercise ID / key:** `low-row-rack`
**Standardized English name:** **Seated Cable Row, close neutral V-handle, pulled to the lower abdomen, upright torso**
**Alternative/common names:** Low Row · Seated Row · Close-Grip Cable Row · V-Bar Row
**Exercise category:** horizontal pull
**Equipment:** low cable pulley + flat bench + foot platform, close-grip **V/triangle handle** (two parallel neutral grips). Not a machine row, not a chest-supported row.
**New record created by split:** no.

## B. Starting Position
**Body orientation:** seated on a flat bench, facing the low pulley, legs extended forward with feet braced on a footplate.
**Torso angle:** upright, roughly vertical, with only a small forward reach at the stretch. Not a 45° hinge.
**Back support/position:** unsupported; neutral spine held actively. App cue 3 forbids rounding at the stretch.
**Shoulder position:** protracted with a slight forward reach at the stretch; depressed and retracted at the finish. The video's central ❌/✅ pair is exactly this — red highlight on the rear delt/upper trap vs green on the lat.
**Elbow position:** tucked close to the ribs throughout — the defining detail of this variation.
**Hip position:** ~90°, seated.
**Knee position:** slightly bent, not locked.
**Foot position:** flat on the footplate, hip-width, braced.

## C. Grip / Contact
**Grip type:** **neutral** (palms facing each other) on the V-handle; the video marks a **thumbless grip** as ✅ against a thumb-wrapped grip as ❌.
**Grip width:** narrow — hands roughly 15–20 cm apart, fixed by the handle.
**Hand position:** at abdominal height at the finish.
**Handle/bar type:** close-grip V/triangle handle.
**Other body contact points:** bench seat and footplate — the feet take the reaction force.

## D. Movement
**Start position:** arms extended forward, cable taut, scapulae protracted, torso upright or leaning very slightly forward.
**End position:** handle at the **lower abdomen**, elbows behind the torso, scapulae retracted.
**Main joint actions:** shoulder extension (not horizontal abduction — the elbows stay in), elbow flexion, scapular retraction and depression.
**Movement path:** the handle travels horizontally backward and slightly downward toward the navel.
**Elbow path:** back and tight to the ribs, brushing the torso. This is what makes it a "low row" rather than a mid-back row.
**Shoulder/scapula behavior:** protract to reach, then depress and retract; the video's coaching is that if you feel the rear delt/upper trap you have flared the elbow and lost the lat.
**Hip/knee behavior:** static — no torso rocking, no leg drive.

## E. Resistance Mechanics
**Resistance source:** cable, selectorized weight stack.
**Direction of resistance:** horizontally forward, back toward the low pulley.
**Cable direction:** **low and horizontal, from directly in front at roughly hip/abdomen height.** This low angle is what puts the resistance line under the humerus and biases shoulder extension.
**Machine path:** N/A — free on the cable.
**Resistance relative to body:** horizontal into the abdomen; the upright torso keeps the resistance arm at the shoulder constant through the pull.

## F. Range of Motion
**Approximate ROM:** full forward reach with scapular protraction → handle touching the lower abdomen.
**Deep stretch position:** arms fully extended forward, scapulae protracted, lats lengthened.
**Peak contraction position:** handle at the abdomen with the elbows behind the torso line.
**Full / partial / intentionally restricted:** full, with the stretch reached by allowing protraction — no lumbar rounding to gain extra reach (app cue 3).

## G. Execution Details
**Unilateral / bilateral:** bilateral · **Alternating / simultaneous:** simultaneous
**Open / closed kinetic chain:** open
**Tempo shown:** not determinable
**Special technique details:** thumbless neutral grip · elbows glued to the ribs · pull to the navel not the chest · upright torso, no rocking.

## H. Critical Variation Details
What separates this from every other row in the program: the **cable comes from below and in front at abdomen height**, not horizontally at chest height (`seated-row-heavy`) and not from overhead (`lat-pulldown`). The **elbows stay tucked** and the handle finishes at the **lower abdomen**, which makes the shoulder action mostly **extension** rather than horizontal abduction. The **narrow neutral V-handle** fixes the hands close together — a wide pronated bar on the same station would be a different exercise. The torso is **upright and unsupported**, so trunk musculature works isometrically, unlike the chest-supported machine row. There is no hip hinge and no body English.

## I. Confidence
**HIGH CONFIDENCE:** seated cable row on a low pulley with a flat bench and footplate; close neutral V-handle; upright unsupported torso; elbows tucked; pull to the lower abdomen; bilateral; open chain; thumbless grip taught as correct.
**MODERATE CONFIDENCE:** that Tavor's setup is the same improvised bench-and-low-pulley arrangement rather than a dedicated row station — the folder name "חתירה מלמטה בכלוב" (low row in the rack) matches the video's setup well, which is why this is moderate rather than low.
**LOW CONFIDENCE:** exact handle model.
**UNKNOWN:** tempo, reps, stack setting in his gym.

# EXERCISE 2 — Machine Preacher Curl

## A. Identification
**App name after audit:** `כפיפת מרפקים בספה` (unchanged)
**Previous app name:** `יד קדמית בספה`
**Exercise ID / key:** `preacher-curl`
**Standardized English name:** **Seated Machine Preacher Curl (selectorized, independent rotating handles, inclined arm pad)**
**Alternative/common names:** Preacher Curl Machine · Machine Biceps Curl · Scott Curl (machine)
**Exercise category:** elbow flexion (single-joint), humerus fixed in slight shoulder flexion
**Equipment:** selectorized preacher curl machine — inclined arm pad, seat, weight-stack housing, **two independent rotating handles** (one per hand).
**New record created by split:** no. See section K / MANUAL REVIEW — one of the two attached videos shows a **barbell** preacher curl, a different equipment variation.

## B. Starting Position
**Body orientation:** seated at the machine, chest against the top of the pad, both upper arms lying flat on the inclined pad.
**Torso angle:** upright to slightly forward, leaning into the pad.
**Back support/position:** the chest/torso rests against the pad top; the spine is not loaded.
**Shoulder position:** fixed in slight flexion by the pad — the shoulder must not move. Video 01's ❌/✅ pair marks shoulder travel (red arrow) as the fault.
**Elbow position:** planted on the pad and staying planted — video 01 highlights the elbows in green with inward arrows; ❌ is elbows lifting or sliding.
**Hip position:** seated ~90°.
**Knee position:** ~90°, feet down.
**Foot position:** flat on the floor for bracing only.

## C. Grip / Contact
**Grip type:** semi-supinated to supinated on rotating handles (the handle rotates, so the wrist can settle).
**Grip width:** fixed by the machine, roughly shoulder-width.
**Hand position:** **wrist held straight, in line with the forearm** — this is the entire teaching point of video 02: ❌ wrist bent back (red angled overlay) vs ✅ wrist neutral (green straight overlay).
**Handle/bar type:** two independent rotating machine handles.
**Other body contact points:** upper arms and chest on the inclined pad, seat, floor.

## D. Movement
**Start position:** arms extended down the pad, elbows near full extension, handles low.
**End position:** elbows flexed, handles curled up toward the shoulders, short of the point where tension is lost.
**Main joint actions:** elbow flexion; forearm supination is fixed by the handle; the shoulder is static.
**Movement path:** the handles arc up along the machine's fixed path around the elbow axis.
**Elbow path:** none — the elbow is the pinned pivot on the pad.
**Shoulder/scapula behavior:** static. Any shoulder travel means the pad has been left.
**Hip/knee behavior:** N/A.

## E. Resistance Mechanics
**Resistance source:** machine lever driven by a **selectorized weight stack** (`usesPlates: false`, `weightIncrementKg: 5`, 32 kg — all stack-shaped).
**Direction of resistance:** pulls the handles back down toward extension along the lever arc.
**Cable direction:** N/A.
**Machine path:** **fixed arc**; independent handles that rotate at the grip. Pivot is at the elbow line, under the pad. Whether the two arms are mechanically linked could not be established.
**Resistance relative to body:** the arm pad tilts the humerus forward so that the resistance is heaviest near the stretched (extended) position — the opposite of a standing curl, where the peak is around 90°.
**Machine classification:** selectorized · fixed arc · independent rotating handles · pivot at the elbow · linked/independent arms UNKNOWN.

## F. Range of Motion
**Approximate ROM:** near-full elbow extension → full flexion.
**Deep stretch position:** bottom, elbow nearly straight, arm flat on the pad. App cue 2 asks for "almost full stretch"; cue 4 asks for a one-second pause there.
**Peak contraction position:** top of the curl.
**Full / partial / intentionally restricted:** full ROM, deliberately **not resting at the top** (cue 3) — i.e. the top is cut slightly short to hold tension.

## G. Execution Details
**Unilateral / bilateral:** bilateral (both handles at once) · **Alternating / simultaneous:** simultaneous
**Open / closed kinetic chain:** open
**Tempo shown:** not determinable, but a one-second pause at the bottom is prescribed.
**Special technique details:** armpit pressed into the pad and not sliding · wrist straight, never bent back · pause in the stretched position · no rest at the top.

## H. Critical Variation Details
The **inclined pad is the whole exercise** — it fixes the humerus in slight flexion, which shortens the biceps at the shoulder and makes the stretched position the hardest part. That is the opposite loading profile from a standing curl. Compared with the other biceps work in the same session: `hammer-curl` is a **neutral-grip standing dumbbell** curl with a free elbow, and `behind-body-cable-curl` puts the elbow **behind** the body line. This one puts it **in front** and locks it. Grip is supinated/semi-supinated on rotating handles, not neutral. The **machine's cam** — not gravity — sets the resistance curve, which is the main mechanical difference from the barbell preacher curl shown in video 01.

## I. Confidence
**HIGH CONFIDENCE:** it is a preacher curl with the upper arms on an inclined pad; elbows pinned; single-joint elbow flexion; wrist kept straight; the stretched position is emphasised; video 02 shows a selectorized machine with independent rotating handles.
**HIGH CONFIDENCE:** video 01 shows a **straight-barbell** preacher curl on a preacher bench (loaded plates marked "40" are clearly visible) — a different equipment variation from the record.
**MODERATE CONFIDENCE:** that Tavor's version is the machine — inferred from `equipment: 'machine'`, `usesPlates: false`, `weightIncrementKg: 5` and a 32 kg load, all of which read as a weight stack rather than a barbell.
**LOW CONFIDENCE:** whether the machine arms are linked or independent; exact grip angle.
**UNKNOWN:** brand/model; the cam's resistance curve; tempo; reps.

**muscleGroup:** `biceps` · **subTarget:** ביצפס · **secondary:** `[]` (isolation)
**equipment:** `machine`
**cues:** 1) בית שחי צמוד לכרית, לא מחליק 2) לרדת עד מתיחה כמעט מלאה — שם הצמיחה 3) לא לנוח למעלה, לשמור מתח 4) לעצור שנייה בתחתית לפני שעולים

# EXERCISE 4 — Behind-the-Body Cable Curl

## A. Identification
**App name after audit:** `כפיפת מרפקים בכבל עם מרפקים מאחורי הגוף` (unchanged)
**Previous app name:** `יד קדמית בפולי עם מרפקים מאחורי הגוף`
**Exercise ID / key:** `behind-body-cable-curl`
**Standardized English name:** **Behind-the-Body Cable Curl (standing in front of a low pulley, elbow behind the torso line)** — *name carried from Tavor's own list; NOT verified against any video.*
**Alternative/common names:** Drag-Behind Cable Curl · Bayesian-style Cable Curl (when done single-arm)
**Exercise category:** elbow flexion (single-joint) with the shoulder held in extension
**Equipment:** cable, low pulley.
**New record created by split:** no.

## B–G. Position, grip, movement, mechanics, ROM, execution
**⚠ There is NO video for this exercise.** The source folder `07. יד קדמית בפולי…` is empty and Tavor's verification document marks B07 `verified: false — אין סרטון, השם משוער בלבד`.
What can be stated comes from the record and is **inference, not observation**:
- Standing **in front of** the pulley so the cable runs behind the body and the **elbow sits behind the torso line** (cue 1) — this puts the shoulder in extension and lengthens the biceps at the shoulder joint.
- The **stretched position is the point of the exercise** and must not be cut short (cue 2).
